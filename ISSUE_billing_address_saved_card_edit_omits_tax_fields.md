Summary: A customer editing a saved card in a Checkout Elements session that calculates automatic tax from billing changes their country from France to the United States. The editor collects only country and ZIP, accepts Save, and then the saved card disappears and the selected payment option is cleared. The customer cannot use or repair that saved card in this checkout and must enter a new card; pressing Confirm produces "Confirmation failed: Something went wrong".

Repro: Create a test customer with a saved card whose billing address contains only `country=FR`, with `allow_redisplay=always`. Create a Checkout Session with `ui_mode=mobile_elements`, `automatic_tax[enabled]=true`, `billing_address_collection=auto`, `customer_update[address]=auto`, that customer, card payments, and taxable line items with `tax_behavior=exclusive`. Enable saved-payment-method editing. The `customer_update` setting is essential: the init response must report `automatic_tax.address_source=billing`, not `customer`. Configure `CheckoutElementsController` with a Payment Element; the French saved card is available and selected. Tap Edit, change the country to United States, enter ZIP `94103`, and tap Save. No street, city, or state inputs are offered. The sheet closes, tax updates, the saved card vanishes, and the screen says "No payment method selected". Tap Confirm to see the failure. The test below creates fresh test objects and supplies the session settings through a local relay to the real playground backend.

Test: [repro_tax_billing_edit.py](bug-bash/billing-address/repro_tax_billing_edit.py), `BillingTaxEditTest.test_saved_card_country_change_collects_us_tax_address`. Build/install and run instructions are in the file's docstring. Reproduced manually and independently by this test on a physical Pixel 6a, Android 16/API 36, at `cf7dd4b99965a4e754abdadacf4f337e764f6e6a`. The test reaches the lost selection and confirmation failure, then fails its regression assertion: "Saved-card editor omitted required US tax fields: Address, City, State" ([result](bug-bash/billing-address/regression-result.txt)). Live backend, no mocked Stripe responses. Evidence: [editor omits tax fields](bug-bash/billing-address/editor-missing-tax-fields.png), [card disappears](bug-bash/billing-address/saved-card-disappeared.png), [confirmation fails](bug-bash/billing-address/confirmation-failed.png), [backend retains the incomplete card](bug-bash/billing-address/backend-after-edit.json), and [device recording](bug-bash/billing-address/tax-billing-edit.mp4).

Elaboration: `DefaultEmbeddedUpdateScreenInteractorFactory` passes the ordinary address collection mode into `DefaultUpdatePaymentMethodInteractor`/`DefaultEditCardDetailsInteractor`; `BillingDetailsForm` hardcodes `requiresBillingAddressForAutomaticTax=false`. With automatic collection, its US form therefore exposes only country and postal code, and `BillingDetailsEntry.isComplete` accepts those two fields. `SavedPaymentMethodMutator.modifyCardPaymentMethod` successfully persists that incomplete address. `ManageLaunchSavedPaymentMethodSelector.syncBillingAfterEdit` updates the billing tax region, and `CheckoutSheetLauncher` applies the returned state and refreshes through `DefaultCheckoutSessionRefresher`/`CheckoutStateLoader`. The reloaded customer passes through `DefaultPaymentMethodFilter`, whose `hasSufficientBillingDetailsForAutomaticTax` check requires US line1, city, state, and postal code. It rejects the very card the editor just accepted; selection reconciliation consequently clears it. The backend retains the saved card, so its disappearance is SDK filtering, not detachment. The editor must honor the same country-specific tax requirements as new-card collection and saved-card eligibility.
